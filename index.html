Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know Before You Start

Do I need technical experience?

No. The workbook was designed for beginners and guides you through each decision using simple explanations, practical exercises and AI prompts.

Do I need an existing business or audience?

No. You can begin with your current skills, experience and knowledge. The workbook helps you identify a useful product opportunity and define the people it can help.

What type of digital product will I create?

The process can help you create a workbook, guide, checklist, template, spreadsheet or another simple digital product that matches the problem you choose to solve.

How much time do I need?

You can work through the blueprint at your own pace. A few focused hours each week are enough to make steady progress without leaving your job or ignoring your responsibilities.

Is this a video course?

No. This is a practical AI-powered workbook. You read, complete the exercises, use the included prompts and build your product as you progress.

Will AI create the entire product for me?

No. AI supports your research, thinking and writing, but you remain responsible for the decisions and the value of the final product. The blueprint shows you how to use AI as a practical assistant rather than as a replacement for your judgment.
